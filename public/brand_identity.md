# Apex Computer Brand Style Guide

## 1. Introduction
Apex Computer is a modern, professional technology company focused on high-performance computing and precision engineering. The brand identity reflects stability, innovation, and peak performance.

## 2. Logo
The logo consists of a stylized letter 'A'. It is designed to be minimalist and geometric.
- **Logo v1:** Geometric clean lines, navy and electric blue.
- **Logo v2:** Abstract dynamic shards, teal and charcoal.
- **Logo v3:** Bold solid peak, electric blue and dark slate.

## 3. Color Palette
- **Primary Color:** Deep Navy (#002D62) - Represents trust and professionalism.
- **Secondary Color:** Electric Blue (#00AEEF) - Represents energy and technology.
- **Accent Color:** Silver/Gray (#C0C0C0) - Represents precision and hardware.
- **Background/Neutral:** Off-white (#F4F4F4) - For clean layouts.

## 4. Typography
- **Headings:** Montserrat (Bold) - Clean, geometric, and modern.
- **Body Text:** Inter (Regular) - High legibility for technical content.
- **Monospace (for code/tech):** JetBrains Mono - Clear and precise.
