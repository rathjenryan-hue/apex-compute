## 4. Suggested Color Palette for Dark Redesign
- **Background:** Deep Charcoal (#121212) or Midnight Navy (#001A33)
- **Primary Accent:** Electric Blue (#00AEEF)
- **Secondary Accent:** Silver/Chrome (#E0E0E0)
- **Text:** Off-white (#F4F4F4) for readability.
